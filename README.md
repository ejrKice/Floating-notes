# Floating Notes

A notes window that floats on top of Zoom, slides and everything else.

**Open it:** https://ejrkice.github.io/floating-notes/

- **Pop out on top**: a small always-on-top notes window (Chrome or Edge on a computer).
- **Install it** from the page to get its own icon and window. Works offline after the first visit.
- Engineering symbols, time stamps, and screenshots: press Win + Shift + S, then Ctrl + V.
- Optional Frutiger Aero mode, sound effects, and a music player for your own MP3/WAV files.
- **Private**: notes, screenshots and music stay in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.
